Our Core Services

Rego Check

Your NSW e-Safety Check (pink slip) done while you wait. If your car passes, the results go electronically to Transport for NSW, so you can renew your rego straight away.

Air Conditioning

Weak A/C in a Sydney summer? We repair and service car air conditioning, including leak checks and re-gassing, so you get cold, fresh air again.

Log Book Servicing

Serviced to your manufacturer’s schedule so your new-car warranty stays protected. We agree the price with you before we start work.
